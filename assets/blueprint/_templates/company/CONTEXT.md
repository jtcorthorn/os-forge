# Company contract

Inputs: company-approved records, PROFILE.md and current task.
Process: distinguish entity, role, source date, current operating truth and historical evidence.
Outputs: company-owned deliverables, decisions and _meta/BITACORA.md.
Human boundary: accountable operator authorizes business actions. Investors do not inherit operator powers.
Sharing boundary: confirm actual recipient permissions before relying on separation. Private management material requires separate controlled storage/mount where needed.
