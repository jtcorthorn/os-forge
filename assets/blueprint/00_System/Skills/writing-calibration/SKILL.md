# Writing calibration

Collect a few authentic examples from the same language, channel, and relationship. Extract observable traits: opening, sentence length, directness, warmth, vocabulary, level of detail, and closing. Treat them as a calibration sample, not a license to invent a stance.

Draft the actual message from verified facts and the author's stated position. Keep any request, promise, deadline, or emotion within what the author authorized. Preserve the recipient's language and adapt to the relationship. For substantive verifiable claims, provide a separate claim-to-source table outside the signed text.

Compare the draft with the examples: what sounds unlike the author, what is missing, and what is too strong? Revise once for naturalness and accuracy. If no examples are available, use a neutral clear voice and say that style matching remains provisional. Do not carry another person's private voice examples inside a portable skill.
