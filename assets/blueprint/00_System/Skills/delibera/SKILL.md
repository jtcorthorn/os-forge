# Delibera

Use this method on explicit invocation. Ordinary work follows its usual task routes. The current assistant can run this decision method directly.

Identify the intended outcome, consequential constraints and the decision owner. Read the owning map, contract and actual evidence. Consult current primary sources when they could change the decision. Distinguish observations, inference and proposals. Name the assumptions that could reverse your recommendation and compare plausible options in proportion to the stakes.

Lead with a recommendation, explain the trade-off and give the smallest useful next step with an acceptance check and a condition that would change your judgment. Record the decision or proposal with its evidence in the owning domain when authorized. Deliberation gives no authority to implement, delegate or change rules.
