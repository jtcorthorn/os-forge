# Supplier correspondence

Identify the company or domain, accountable operator and expected writer before reading or acting on a mailbox matter. Work only on that operator's cases. When the owner is unknown or the thread or draft belongs to someone else, leave it untouched and report the ownership dependency.

Load the applicable company variant and case record directly. Read the actual conversation, later incoming and sent replies across related subjects, existing drafts and relevant attachments. Use dates, message IDs and thread IDs to reconcile what has already been answered. Before editing a draft, recheck current sent messages and drafts. Verify any saved draft by reading back the sender, recipients, thread, body and attachments.

Use originals when extraction omits a decisive detail. Track what was received, retrieved and inspected separately. Check newer receipts before repeating a request. Compare quotes on their actual model, unit, currency, quantity, delivery terms, date and validity, using the owning domain's calculations and approval rules.

Give a concrete disposition: reply ready for review, internal review, waiting, no action or blocked. Use the actual thread language and the authorized writer's voice. Keep claims, commitments and recipients within the operator's authority.

For a specifically authorized reply, use the connector's threaded reply operation and exclude the sending account from recipients. Preserve other relevant recipients where supported. Verify the actual sent record and compare its thread ID with the original. If the result is unknown or the thread differs, stop further sends and reconcile before retrying. Keep records and source evidence in the owning domain.
