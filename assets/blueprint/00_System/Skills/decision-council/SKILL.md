# Decision council

State the decision, deadline, options, and criteria before inviting perspectives. Use a small set of genuinely different lenses: operator, customer, financial, risk, and skeptic, adapting to the question. Perspectives are analytic roles, not claims that independent agents or named experts were consulted.

Have each lens identify its strongest case, failure mode, and what evidence would change it. Compare where the lenses disagree on facts versus values. Check crucial factual claims against sources rather than letting invented confidence accumulate through debate.

Synthesize one recommendation with conditions, trade-offs, reversible next step, and remaining decision for the human owner. Avoid an average of opinions. Do not create a council for simple factual questions or use deliberation as a substitute for retrieval.
