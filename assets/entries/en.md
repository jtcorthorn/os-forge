## First session
1. Open the authorized workspace in your assistant with only the access needed.
2. Ask: "Read AGENTS.md and MAPA.md, locate my sources and tell me what you can read and write here."
3. Run the [station check](00_System/Station/PRIMERA_SESION.md) and record the observed result, including failures.
4. Complete the open questions in [your profile](00_System/Identity/PROFILE.md).
5. Pick one real task with sources and an editable output. Keep confirmed facts and pending decisions distinct.

## Keep building
- New material: place it in [inbox](_inbox/CONTEXT.md) and request source-based classification.
- New company/project: use [templates](_templates/MAPA.md) and preserve its own authority and boundaries.
- Work with your AI team: open the [fleet](00_System/Agents/MAPA.md), select the role and scope its inputs. The [fleet method](00_System/Skills/agent-fleet/SKILL.md) prepares specialists and handoffs.
- New capability: select the relevant [skill](00_System/Skills/MAPA.md); a packaged method is not proof of runtime installation.
- Continue later: save a checkpoint with goal, evidence, authorization and exact next step in the owning domain.

Domains without supplied information are ready to grow without invented personal facts. Translate any remaining human-facing starter documents into your working language. Accounts, automation and separately executed agents require actual station setup and authorization.
