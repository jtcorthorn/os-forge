# Your OS, {{OWNER}}

This workspace brings together your sources, methods and working tools. Start with [Intake](00_System/Intake/MAPA.md), which records current original locations and reading coverage.

## First session
1. Open the authorized workspace in your assistant with only the access needed.
2. Ask: "Read AGENTS.md and MAPA.md, locate my sources and tell me what you can read and write here."
3. Run the [station check](00_System/Station/PRIMERA_SESION.md) and record the observed result, including failures.
4. Complete the open questions in [your profile](00_System/Identity/PROFILE.md).
5. Pick one real task with sources and an editable output. Keep confirmed facts and pending decisions distinct.
6. In a fresh session, ask: "Read the OS entry, find my last result in its owning domain and continue from the saved record." Check discovery without supplying the exact result path.

Start with one assistant and a few files. Prepared roles and empty domains can wait. With no sources supplied, choose a task that does not require invented personal facts. Keep your root private; sharing OS Forge with a relative does not share your records or accounts.

## Keep building
- New material: place it in [inbox](_inbox/CONTEXT.md) and request source-based classification.
- New company/project: use [templates](_templates/MAPA.md) and preserve its own authority and boundaries.
- Work with your AI assistant. Load the [shared method](00_System/Skills/MAPA.md) and the owning domain contract. Use [agent routes](00_System/Agents/MAPA.md) when the work needs specialist judgment, a separate mandate or an authorized handoff.
- New capability: select the relevant [skill](00_System/Skills/MAPA.md); a packaged method is not proof of runtime installation.
- Continue later: save a checkpoint with goal, evidence, authorization and exact next step in the owning domain.
- Review the OS: "Use the [maintenance playbook](00_System/Skills/os-maintenance/SKILL.md) to check my project's routes and handoffs. Tell me what you verified and who can address each finding."
- Assign work: "Use [Agent Fleet](00_System/Skills/agent-fleet/SKILL.md) to propose who should own this task, what access they need and how we'll verify the handoff."

Domains without supplied information are ready to grow without invented personal facts. Translate any remaining human-facing starter documents into your working language. Accounts, automation and separately executed agents require actual station setup and authorization.
